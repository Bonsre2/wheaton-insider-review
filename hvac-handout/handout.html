Potential HVAC Upgrade Option for This Home

Efficient whole-home heating and cooling — without existing ductwork.

Considering Central Air?

Homes without existing ductwork can be difficult and costly to fit for traditional central air. A ductless mini-split system may offer an alternative worth exploring. A mini-split pairs one compact outdoor unit with one or more slim indoor units, delivering both heating and cooling year-round. Because refrigerant lines run through a small opening rather than bulky ducts, no existing ductwork is required—which can make it a practical consideration for older homes. Whether it is the right fit here depends on the property and should be confirmed by a licensed contractor.
