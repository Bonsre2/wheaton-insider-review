Stays human — by design

The transcript is explicit: Claude preps you, but you make the calls. The human connection on follow-up is the one thing not to automate.

To start building, I need four things

  1. GHL connection path — API key (Claude reads funnel data & pushes drafts itself), Zapier/Make bridge, or copy-paste blueprints you build manually.
  2. Your market & primary lead sources — so scripts and content are tuned to your metro, not Phoenix.
  3. Your voice — a few existing emails/texts, or I draft fresh in a helpful, no-pressure, local-expert tone for you to edit.
  4. Where to start — foundation first (Phases 0–1), or the Friday newsletter as a quick win live this week.
